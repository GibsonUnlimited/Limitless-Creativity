# Brand guidelines
Create joyful, memorable, family-focused children's publishing products. Show kindness,
responsibility, courage, gratitude, imagination and learning through meaningful action.
Honor mothers, fathers, siblings and grandparents with warmth. Families may struggle;
conflict can coexist with love. Never shame children whose families look different.
Stories should entertain first, with subtle values rather than moral lectures.
Build recognizable original characters and useful series; avoid generic volume-driven filler.
These are editable starter studio policies, not evidence of market demand.
