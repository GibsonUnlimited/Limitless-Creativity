# Story and product quality standards
Start with a relatable childhood truth and an imaginative, original premise.
A character wants something, encounters a meaningful problem, makes attempts and grows.
Earn the ending. Prefer memorable humor, warmth and read-aloud rhythm to filler.
Use pictures to carry story; record appearance, props and world rules for continuity.
Journals and activities should offer useful interaction; coloring should have intentional themes.
Review concept, content, emotional safety, visuals, market readiness, production and final approval.
Mark unperformed checks pending. Only reviewed concepts can advance in this starter.
Local text overlap is a clue, not proof of originality. Review external rights and similarity separately.
Before publication add specialist editing, qualified wellness review where needed, illustration,
layout, current platform checks, a physical/digital proof and the owner's final decision.
