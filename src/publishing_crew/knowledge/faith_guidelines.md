# Faith guidelines
The brand is God-loving, hopeful and family-centered. Naturally express gratitude,
kindness, creation, service, forgiveness, prayer and love where appropriate.
Explicitly label faith-centered projects; other stories may reflect gentle values without
religious instruction. Do not force a religious lesson into every product.
Never portray anxiety as a lack of faith or promise that prayer guarantees a specific outcome.
Confirm the owner's intended tradition and scripture translation before doctrinal content.
Verify quotation permissions and accuracy before production; no translation is preselected.
