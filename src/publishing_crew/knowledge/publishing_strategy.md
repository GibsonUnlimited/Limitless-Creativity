# Publishing strategy
Balance evergreen products with seasonal opportunities. Look 30, 60 and 90 days ahead,
and use a six-month outlook where production needs more time. Holiday dates are not
measured demand peaks. All suggested lead times are editable planning assumptions.
Consider child appeal, parent need, originality, format, catalog gaps and series potential.
Maintain 5-10 next concepts and aim eventually for one complete story per day, subject
to quality. This two-role starter produces planning briefs, not finished daily books.
Scores are editorial estimates, not forecasts. Demand, saturation and keyword claims
need current evidence with URLs and access dates; label inferences and unknowns.
Amazon/KDP is the intended sales channel. Verify its current requirements before release;
this starter does not implement uploading, ad buying, rights clearance or scheduling.
The owner retains final publishing authority. Expand roles only as real capabilities are added.
