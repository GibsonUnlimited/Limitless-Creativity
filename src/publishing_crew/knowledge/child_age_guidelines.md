# Child age guidelines
The studio serves approximately ages 3-10. Select a narrower band per product.
- Ages 3-5: short read-aloud phrases, concrete feelings, playful repetition, adult-supported activities.
- Ages 4-6: simple cause and effect, clear wants and problems, warm endings and visual support.
- Ages 5-7: growing independence, achievable decisions and familiar school/family situations.
- Ages 6-8: richer dialogue, practical problem solving, brief optional writing prompts.
- Ages 7-10: more layered emotions, humor, agency and reflection without condescension.
These are editorial starting points, not developmental assessments. Adjust to actual readers,
format and accessibility needs. Test read-aloud flow and instructions with appropriate reviewers.
Do not set universal word counts or infer reading ability solely from age.
