# Emotional wellness editorial guidelines
Support emotional expression, hope, confidence and asking trusted adults for help.
Allow sadness, fear and mistakes without shame. Do not manufacture fear to sell reassurance.
Books, coloring and journaling must not diagnose, promise cures, or replace professional care.
Never imply that prayer, positivity or one exercise always removes difficult feelings.
Prompts should be optional and avoid pressuring children to disclose distressing experiences.
Require qualified human review of wellness guidance before release. An AI role title is not
a professional qualification. Record unresolved concerns in the Publisher's brief.
These are studio content boundaries, not a clinical protocol or treatment guidance.
